# High-Tech Low-Lives

*A Fate Core campaign set in the grimy underbelly of humanity's furthest frontier.*

---

## The Setting

It's 1995. Japan won the Second World War. Nuclear weapons never worked. Through their own Operation Paperclip, Japan recruited the world's brightest minds and accelerated technology beyond anything the old world expected. Humanity reached the outer planets.

**Space Station Ganges** hangs in orbit above Jupiter — overcrowded, corrupt, and teetering on the edge of a gang war. The Empire of Nihon's authority barely reaches this far out, leaving a power vacuum that criminal organizations have been happy to fill. The Shogunate controls commerce. The Blackuza controls the drug trade. Everyone else survives however they can.

## The Characters

Three people scraping by at the bottom of a very tall station.

### Brenjo
A freelance back-alley surgeon who is, by most accounts, terrible at his job. A near-fatal airlock blowout left him rattled and deeply afraid of the void just outside the hull. He keeps his brother Toomba afloat and gets his medical supplies from an unlikely source.

### Chuuya Nakahara
A high-functioning drug addict and talented chemist working for the Blackuza. Chuuya's social circle tends to grow wherever his latest synthesis goes. He doesn't always consider how his experiments affect others — a lesson Brenjo learned the hard way.

### Shigeru Miyamoto
A black-market smuggler who knows every maintenance corridor, airlock, and shady contact on Ganges. He survived his first arms deal by sheer luck, field-tests Chuuya's pharmaceuticals for a steady supply, and is quietly in love with the wrong person's wife.

## The Substances

Two drugs worth knowing about if you spend any time on Ganges.

- **Opibiotics** — Tightly controlled by the Shogunate and used as a tool of population management
- **LSD-728** — A harder, longer-lasting variant of LSD. Effects last 23 hours. Chuuya's specialty.
