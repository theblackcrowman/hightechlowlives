# Player Characters

Each character has five aspects: a **High Concept**, a **Trouble**, and three aspects generated through the Phase Trio (shared backstory with other characters). Aspects can be **invoked** by spending a Fate point for a +2 bonus or a reroll. The GM can **compel** an aspect to complicate the character's situation, offering a Fate point the player can accept or buy off by spending one of their own. *(Fate Core, "Aspects and Fate Points")*

## Brenjo

- Freelance Back-Alley Surgeon *(High Concept)*
  - *Invoke* for any medical task, to pass as a practitioner, or to source medical equipment through back channels.
  - *Compel* to attract desperate or dangerous clients, draw scrutiny from authorities, or be called on for help at the worst possible moment.

- Terrible at his job *(Trouble)*
  - *Invoke* when catastrophic failure is somehow advantageous — creating a distraction, producing an unexpected outcome, or lowering an enemy's expectations.
  - *Compel* to have medical attempts go dangerously wrong, equipment malfunction at a critical moment, or patients distrust and abandon him mid-treatment.

- Scared of the great black void *(Aspect — Phase Trio)*
  - A random airlock blew out while Brenjo was standing near it due to negligent maintenance, making him on edge and causing a fear of space.
  - *Invoke* when fear-driven desperation fuels an adrenaline surge, or to justify extreme measures to avoid an EVA.
  - *Compel* to freeze near airlocks, refuse a necessary spacewalk, or panic when the void becomes unavoidable.

- Medical goods are supplied by [Shiggy](#shigeru-miyamoto) *(Aspect — Phase Trio)*
  - Brenjo nursed [Shigeru Miyamoto](#shigeru-miyamoto) back to health after intervening and causing his attackers to flee, he now gets his medical supplies from [Shigeru](#shigeru-miyamoto).
  - *Invoke* to access hard-to-find medical supplies or call on Shigeru for a favour.
  - *Compel* when Shigeru's criminal dealings implicate Brenjo, or supply lines are severed at a critical moment.

- [Toomba](HTLLNPCs.md#toomba) relies on me *(Aspect — Phase Trio)*
  - At the venue I was the surgeon for, [Chuuya Nakahara](#chuuya-nakahara) caused a massive disturbance, causing it to be shut down, and my brother [Toomba](HTLLNPCs.md#toomba) now relies on me to live.
  - *Invoke* when Toomba's dependence fuels extraordinary effort or justifies taking a serious risk.
  - *Compel* when Toomba's needs pull Brenjo away from the mission, or when someone uses Toomba as leverage against him.

## Chuuya Nakahara

- Drug synthesizer for the [Blackuza](HTLLOrganizations.md#blackuza) *(High Concept)*
  - *Invoke* for chemistry and synthesis tasks, navigating Blackuza territory, or calling on criminal contacts.
  - *Compel* when the Blackuza make demands, his ties expose him to danger, or his reputation as a drug cook precedes him in the wrong company.

- High functioning drug addict *(Trouble)*
  - *Invoke* when a drug-heightened state provides a genuine edge — enhanced focus, suppressed fear, or unusual lateral thinking.
  - *Compel* when addiction affects judgment, creates dependency complications, or makes it impossible to function without a fix at a critical moment.

- Drugs make it easy to make friends *(Aspect — Phase Trio)*
  - Took my first successful synthesis of [LSD-728](HTLLSpaceDrugs.md#lsd-728) to a concert where it was shared with a ton of people. Later got lost in [Blackuza](HTLLOrganizations.md#blackuza) territory. Shared drugs with them in exchange for safety and ended up being recruited.
  - *Invoke* to create social openings quickly, establish rapport with strangers, or navigate a new group by offering product.
  - *Compel* when generosity with drugs creates unexpected obligations, dangerous entanglements, or situations that spiral beyond Chuuya's control.

- Does not consider limitations of others *(Aspect — Phase Trio)*
  - Gave [Brenjo](#brenjo) drugs to take the edge off of his harrowing experience with the airlock. My drugs only made things worse.
  - *Invoke* when pushing someone beyond their normal limits is exactly what is needed, or when Chuuya's obliviousness lets him ask for things others would never dare request.
  - *Compel* when Chuuya causes harm through disregard for others' tolerances, triggers a conflict by demanding too much, or fails to notice someone is in genuine danger.

- [Shigeru](#shigeru-miyamoto) often field tests drugs for me in exchange for free drugs *(Aspect — Phase Trio)*
  - Provide experimental pharmaceuticals to [Shigeru](#shigeru-miyamoto) for free on the condition that he field tests them.
  - *Invoke* to obtain rapid real-world results on a synthesis, or to call on Shigeru as a willing subject.
  - *Compel* when a field test goes badly wrong, Shigeru's condition becomes Chuuya's responsibility, or the arrangement draws unwanted attention.

## Shigeru Miyamoto

- Back-Alley Smuggler *(High Concept)*
  - *Invoke* for moving contraband, navigating black markets, leveraging criminal contacts, or slipping through checkpoints undetected.
  - *Compel* when his criminal reputation closes legitimate doors, attracts law enforcement, or makes him a target for rivals.

- In love with [Oda Sakigowa](HTLLNPCs.md#oda-sakigowa)'s wife *(Trouble)*
  - *Invoke* when this love provides extraordinary motivation or unexpected access.
  - *Compel* whenever the situation puts him near Sakigowa, forces a choice between self-preservation and his feelings, or when the secret is at risk of surfacing.

- Survive by the skin of my teeth *(Aspect — Phase Trio)*
  - During first foray into illegal arms purchasing, was robbed by a gang, but managed to survive against all odds.
  - *Invoke* when Shigeru needs one last desperate escape — a narrow opening, an unlikely break, or an implausible survival.
  - *Compel* to have him escape a situation but at significant cost — injured, robbed, indebted, or with his cover blown.

- Hard Partying Degenerate *(Aspect — Phase Trio)*
  - Took too much [LSD](HTLLSpaceDrugs.md#lsd-728) at a concert, and got the place shut down from his antics.
  - *Invoke* to access seedy venues, connect with underground contacts, or use his reputation for recklessness to get through doors others couldn't.
  - *Compel* when his excesses create collateral damage, draw the wrong kind of attention, or leave him compromised at a critical moment.

- Know Ganges inside and out *(Aspect — Phase Trio)*
  - I know the maintenance engineers responsible for airlocks on Ganges.
  - *Invoke* for navigation, locating hidden passages, finding people or places on the station, or leveraging knowledge of its infrastructure.
  - *Compel* when his reputation as someone who knows everyone means the wrong people come looking for him, or when he is expected to have information he doesn't.
